About Us

Welcome to our cozy little corner of caffeine! ☕

This coffee business is not just about selling coffee — it’s about brewing dreams. We started this café as a part of our inspiring journey from “1 Rupee to 1 Crore”, where we explore different businesses, ideas, and hustles with nothing but passion and vision.

Founded by Chetan Thakre, this coffee shop reflects his belief that real success comes from learning by doing. From creating YouTube content to trying hands-on businesses, every step is about growth, learning, and sharing.

So, every cup you see here is not just coffee — it’s a step in our journey, and we’re super excited to have you with us on this flavorful adventure. ☕✨
